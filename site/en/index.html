PhotoDelete

A simpler way to clean your iPhone photo library. Pick a category, swipe left to mark for deletion, right to skip, up to favorite — then confirm all at once.

Clean your library with clear, simple gestures.

PhotoDelete skips complex rules and keeps core cleanup outside the paywall. The current release focuses on the essentials: categorize, swipe, queue, and batch-confirm.

← Swipe left to delete

Photos aren't deleted instantly. They go into a candidate queue first, then you confirm all at once before leaving.

→ Swipe right to skip

Don't want to deal with a photo? Swipe right and move on — nothing changes in your library.

↑ Swipe up to favorite

Add photos to your favorites candidate list. Batch-confirm it together with deletions at the end.

✓ Core cleanup is free

The main cleanup flow is free. The one-time supporter unlock adds long-term stats, history, and themes.

01

Choose a scope

Enter from All Photos, Videos, Screenshots, Favorites, or Timeline to focus on a specific set.

02

Swipe through each photo

Use swipe gestures or the bottom toolbar to undo, favorite, mark for deletion, or skip.

03

Confirm when done

The Done button in the bottom-right walks you through a confirmation flow, so you always know how to exit.

No photos leave your phone.

PhotoDelete uses the iOS Photos framework to read and organize your library. All cleaning decisions, candidate lists, and batch operations happen on-device. No account needed, nothing uploaded to any server. Read the full privacy policy
